about doctor

Dr. Ayaan Hassan Ali is a highly trained pediatrician specializing in the healthcare, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of illnesses affecting infants, children, and adolescents. With strong clinical knowledge and a compassionate approach, she is dedicated to providing safe, supportive, and high-quality medical care for children at every stage of development. Dr. Ayaan completed her medical education at Jamhuriya University of Science and Technology, where she developed a strong foundation in medicine and clinical practice. She later pursued advanced training in Pediatrics, gaining specialized expertise in child development, childhood illnesses, newborn care, and preventive pediatric medicine. Throughout her professional career, Dr. Ayaan has gained experience in newborn care, childhood infections, vaccinations, nutritional assessment, growth monitoring, respiratory conditions, and general pediatric emergencies. Her approach combines modern pediatric medicine with careful examination, clear communication, and compassionate care. She has a particular interest in child development, newborn health, childhood nutrition, preventive healthcare, vaccination, respiratory illnesses, and the management of common pediatric conditions. She believes that early diagnosis, regular health checkups, proper nutrition, and a supportive environment are essential for healthy childhood development. Known for her patience, kindness, and professionalism, Dr. Ayaan works closely with children and their families to understand their concerns and provide personalized medical guidance. She believes that parents should be well informed about their child's health and actively involved in their care and development.

Currently, she serves as:
Pediatrics Specialist
Child Healthcare Consultant
Pediatric and Newborn Care Specialist
Dr. Ayaan Hassan Ali's professional journey reflects a strong commitment to children's health, continuous medical learning, and compassionate patient care. Through her dedication and clinical practice, she aims to support healthy childhood development and provide families with trusted, high-quality pediatric healthcare.
