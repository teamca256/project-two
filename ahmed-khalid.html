about doctor

Dr. Ahmed Khalid Hassan is a highly trained General Surgeon dedicated to providing safe, effective, and patient-centered surgical care. He later pursued advanced training in General Surgery, gaining specialized expertise in a wide range of surgical procedures and emergency surgical care. Throughout his professional career, Dr. Ahmed has gained experience in abdominal surgery, hernia repair, gallbladder procedures, wound management, minor surgical procedures, and emergency surgery. He has a particular interest in minimally invasive surgery, abdominal conditions, hernia treatment, gallbladder disease, wound care, and emergency surgical procedures. Known for his professionalism, precision, and calm approach, Dr. Ahmed takes the time to explain treatment options and surgical procedures to his patients. He believes that patients should feel informed, respected, and comfortable throughout every stage of their surgical care.
