about doctor

Dr. Abdirahman Omar Yusuf is a highly trained cardiologist specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases affecting the heart and cardiovascular system. With strong clinical knowledge and a patient-centered approach, he is dedicated to providing comprehensive, compassionate, and high-quality cardiac care. Dr. Abdirahman completed his medical education at Jamhuriya University of Science and Technology, where he developed a strong foundation in medicine and clinical practice. He later pursued advanced training in Cardiology, gaining specialized expertise in cardiovascular diagnosis, heart disease management, and modern cardiac care. Throughout his professional career, Dr. Abdirahman has gained experience in managing conditions such as hypertension, coronary artery disease, heart failure, cardiac arrhythmias, and other cardiovascular disorders. His approach combines modern medical knowledge with careful assessment, accurate diagnosis, and personalized treatment plans. He has a particular interest in preventive cardiology, hypertension management, heart failure, coronary artery disease, cardiac rhythm disorders, and cardiovascular risk assessment. He believes that early detection, regular heart checkups, healthy lifestyle choices, and appropriate treatment are essential for maintaining long-term heart health. Known for his professionalism, patience, and attention to detail, Dr. Abdirahman takes time to listen to his patients and explain their heart conditions and treatment options clearly. He believes that effective cardiac care requires trust, communication, and active patient involvement.

Currently, he serves as:
Cardiology Specialist
Cardiovascular Care Consultant
Heart Health Specialist
Dr. Abdirahman Omar Yusuf's professional journey reflects a strong commitment to cardiovascular excellence, continuous medical learning, and compassionate patient care. Through his clinical practice and dedication to heart health, he aims to help patients prevent cardiovascular disease, manage existing conditions, and achieve healthier lives.
