about doctor

Dr. Hodan Mohamed Noor is a highly trained dermatologist specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of skin, hair, and nail conditions. With strong clinical knowledge and a patient-centered approach, she is dedicated to providing safe, effective, and personalized dermatological care for patients of all ages. Dr. Hodan completed her medical education at Jamhuriya University of Science and Technology, where she developed a strong foundation in medicine and clinical practice. She later pursued advanced training in Dermatology, gaining specialized expertise in common and complex skin conditions, cosmetic dermatology, and skin health management. Throughout her professional career, Dr. Hodan has gained experience in diagnosing and treating conditions such as acne, eczema, psoriasis, skin infections, allergic skin reactions, pigmentation disorders, and hair and nail problems. Her approach combines modern dermatological knowledge with careful assessment, clear communication, and individualized treatment plans. She has a particular interest in acne management, skin pigmentation, hair loss, eczema, psoriasis, skin allergies, and preventive skin care. She believes that understanding the underlying cause of a skin condition is essential for providing effective treatment and achieving long-term results. Known for her professionalism, patience, and compassionate approach, Dr. Hodan takes the time to understand each patient's concerns and provides clear guidance about their treatment options. She also emphasizes patient education and healthy skincare habits as important parts of maintaining long-term skin health.
