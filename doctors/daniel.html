About Doctor

Dr. Daniel Michael Carter is a highly trained orthopedic surgeon specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of conditions affecting the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and other parts of the musculoskeletal system. With strong clinical knowledge and a patient-centered approach, he is dedicated to helping patients restore mobility, reduce pain, and improve their overall quality of life.


Dr. Daniel completed his medical education at the University of Michigan, where he developed a strong foundation in medicine and clinical practice. He later pursued advanced training in Orthopedic Surgery, gaining specialized expertise in musculoskeletal conditions, sports injuries, joint disorders, and modern orthopedic procedures.


Throughout his professional career, Dr. Daniel has gained experience in fracture management, joint injuries, sports-related injuries, arthritis treatment, ligament and tendon disorders, and orthopedic surgical procedures. His approach combines modern medical and surgical techniques with careful diagnosis, personalized treatment plans, and compassionate patient care.


He has a particular interest in sports injuries, knee and shoulder conditions, joint replacement, fracture care, arthritis management, and minimally invasive orthopedic procedures. He believes that accurate diagnosis, appropriate treatment, and rehabilitation are essential for helping patients regain strength, mobility, and independence.


Known for his professionalism, precision, and supportive approach, Dr. Daniel takes time to understand each patient's condition and explain available treatment options clearly. He also emphasizes rehabilitation, injury prevention, and patient education as important parts of successful orthopedic care.


Currently, he serves as:
Orthopedic Surgery Specialist
Musculoskeletal Care Consultant
Sports Injury Specialist

Dr. Daniel Michael Carter's professional journey reflects a strong commitment to orthopedic excellence, continuous medical learning, and patient-centered care. Through his clinical practice and dedication to improving mobility and physical health, he aims to help patients return to active, healthy, and independent lives.
