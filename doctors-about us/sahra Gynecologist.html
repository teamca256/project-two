About doctor

Drs.Sahra Abdullahi Warsam is a highly trained Somali Obstetrics & Gynecology specialist dedicated to providing comprehensive healthcare for women at every stage of life. With strong clinical knowledge and a patient-centered approach, she focuses on providing safe, compassionate, and high-quality care for women and mothers.

Dr. Sahra completed her medical education at Jamhuriya University of Science and Technology, where she developed a strong foundation in general medicine and clinical practice. She later pursued advanced training in Obstetrics & Gynecology, gaining specialized knowledge in women's reproductive health, pregnancy care, childbirth, and gynecological conditions.


Throughout her professional career, Dr. Sahra has gained experience in antenatal care, maternal health, reproductive health, gynecological diagnosis, family planning, and postnatal care. Her clinical approach combines modern medical knowledge with careful assessment, clear communication, and compassionate treatment.


She has a particular interest in maternal and fetal health, pregnancy management, women's reproductive health, menstrual disorders, infertility care, and preventive gynecological services. She believes that every woman deserves personalized healthcare, respectful communication, and professional medical support.


Known for her professionalism, patience, and compassionate nature, Dr. Sahra works closely with her patients to understand their individual needs and provide appropriate treatment and guidance. She also believes in educating women about their health and encouraging regular medical checkups for early detection and prevention of health problems.


Currently, she serves as:
Obstetrics & Gynecology Specialist
Women's Health Consultant
Maternal and Reproductive Health Specialist
Dr. Sahra Abdullahi Warsam's professional journey reflects a strong commitment to women's health,
medical excellence, continuous learning, and compassionate patient care. Through her dedication and clinical practice, she aims to improve maternal and reproductive healthcare services and support healthier women and families throughout Somalia.
