about doctor

Dr. Mohamed Ahmed Abdi is a highly trained Somali neurologist specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of neurological disorders. With extensive clinical experience and a strong commitment to patient-centered care, he is dedicated to helping patients understand and manage conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, nerves, and nervous system. Dr. Mohamed completed his medical education at Jamhuriya University of Science and Technology, where he developed a strong foundation in medicine and clinical practice. He later pursued advanced training in Neurology, gaining specialized knowledge in the assessment and treatment of a wide range of neurological conditions. Throughout his professional career, Dr. Mohamed has gained experience in clinical neurology, patient assessment, neurological diagnosis, emergency neurological care, and long-term management of neurological disorders. His approach combines modern medical knowledge with careful diagnosis, clear communication, and compassionate patient care. He has a particular interest in conditions such as stroke, epilepsy, headaches and migraine, movement disorders, peripheral nerve disorders, and other diseases affecting the nervous system. He believes that early diagnosis, accurate treatment, and continuous patient support are essential to achieving better health outcomes. Known for his professionalism, patience, and dedication to his patients, Dr. Mohamed focuses on providing individualized care based on each patient's specific condition and needs. He also believes in educating patients and their families so they can better understand their condition and participate actively in the treatment process.
